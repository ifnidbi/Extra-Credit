1. Draft the introduction/goals, related work, course connections, and design/schematics sections for the 10–12 page report. Explain why Python/Flask and MySQL fit the course and show the updated diagram plus roles and major data paths.

2. Be clear about what a student has to do to sign up today, and why one SQL view needs two pieces in MySQL.
