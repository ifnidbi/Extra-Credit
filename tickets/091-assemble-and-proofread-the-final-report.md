# 091 · Assemble and proofread the final report

**Estimate** 60 minutes · **Stage** Delivery  
**Prerequisites** #89, #90

## In brief

Turn individual drafts into one complete team submission.

## Technical steps

1. Combine sections in a 10–12 page Times 11 report with abstract, introduction, related work, design, implementation, results, conclusions, future work, references and contribution matrix. Verify schematics and every required query result snapshot.
2. Check page count, captions, citations and contribution accuracy with all team members. Keep the course copy in the team's private files.

## In first-time-coder language

1. Put the writing in one document. Give it a short summary up front, a clear ending, a list of sources and a table of who did what.
2. Read it together and check the page count, font, pictures and references before making the final export.

## Check your work

- [ ] A 10–12 page Times 11 team report has the required sections and readable diagrams/screenshots.
- [ ] All team members confirm the contribution matrix and review the final export.
