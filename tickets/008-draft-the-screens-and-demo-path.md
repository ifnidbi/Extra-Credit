# 008 · Draft the screens and demo path

**Estimate** 30 minutes · **Stage** Practice  
**Prerequisites** None. Start any time.

## In brief

Sketch what a visitor, student, and admin will see.

## Technical steps

1. In `docs/demo-outline.md`, storyboard catalog, details, login, dashboard, inquiries, the two reports pages, and pickup weather. Label persona and action for each screen.
2. Write a short happy path from browsing through creating and accepting an inquiry, then a separate admin path for reports.

## In first-time-coder language

1. Draw rough boxes for the important pages. Write who can open each one and what they can do there.
2. Tell a short story of one student selling and another buying, then show what the admin can demonstrate.

## Check your work

- [ ] The sketch covers all pages named in the technical section and differentiates three access levels.
- [ ] The buyer, seller, and admin actions agree with the architecture.
